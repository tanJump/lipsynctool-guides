# Rumpelstiltskin AI: Personalize a Dance Template

Rumpelstiltskin AI uses two portraits to personalize a fixed
fairy-tale dance template.

One person is assigned to the dancer and the other to the spectator.

[Open Rumpelstiltskin AI](https://lipsynctool.com/rumpelstiltskin-ai)

## What you need

- One clear portrait for the dancer.
- One clear portrait for the spectator.
- Permission to use both photos.
- A LipSyncTool account with sufficient credits.

Use one adult per photo. Choose clear, well-lit faces without
obstructions or heavy filters.

## Step 1: Preview the template

Play the reference template with sound.

The template shows the dance, setting, camera shots and
matching soundtrack used by this workflow.

The preview is source footage, not a personalized generation result.

## Step 2: Assign the two roles

Upload the dancer portrait to the left slot.

Upload the spectator portrait to the right slot.

Use the swap control if you want to exchange their roles
before generating.

## Step 3: Generate your video

Review the displayed credit cost and submit the generation.

The tool uses a fixed 6-second landscape template.
Its matching soundtrack is included in the workflow.

This feature does not offer custom reference videos,
custom songs or adjustable template duration.

## Step 4: Review and download

Check both people in the result before sharing.

Pay attention to:

- Facial likeness.
- Hair and clothing details.
- Identity consistency across shots.
- The soundtrack and playback.

Download the MP4 when the task is complete.
Use Video history to revisit your generation tasks.

## What results should I expect?

This is an early version.

The AI may preserve one identity better than the other,
change clothing details or interpret the reference portraits
differently across shots.

A perfect two-person replacement is not guaranteed.

## How is this different from Hotel Lobby AI?

Rumpelstiltskin AI personalizes a fixed dance-and-spectator scene.

[Hotel Lobby AI](hotel-lobby-ai.md) creates a new musical duet
from two portraits and performance settings.
