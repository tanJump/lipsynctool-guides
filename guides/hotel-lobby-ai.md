# Hotel Lobby AI: Create a Duet from Two Portraits

Hotel Lobby AI creates a new musical video from two portraits
and preset performance directions.

[Open Hotel Lobby AI](https://lipsynctool.com/hotel-lobby-ai)

## What you need

- Two clear portraits, with one adult in each image.
- Permission to use both photos.
- A LipSyncTool account with sufficient credits.

Use JPG, PNG or WebP images up to 10 MB each.
Choose well-lit photos without sunglasses, face coverings
or heavy filters.

## Step 1: Upload the two portraits

Add one portrait to each performer slot.

Use separate portraits rather than a group photo.
The images provide visual references for the two performers.

## Step 2: Choose the performance

Select a preset style and voice direction.

Available options include different musical moods and
female, male or mixed voice directions. Beat-only and
silent options are also available.

These settings guide the AI model. They do not guarantee
an exact voice, speaker assignment or performance timing.

## Step 3: Choose the video format

Choose a duration:

- 5 seconds
- 10 seconds
- 15 seconds

Choose an aspect ratio:

- 16:9 for landscape videos
- 9:16 for portrait videos

The current tool uses 720p output.

## Step 4: Generate and review

Check the displayed credit cost, then submit your generation.

When the result is ready, review:

- Whether both performers resemble their portraits.
- The performance and mouth movement.
- The generated voices and audio.
- Any unexpected visual changes.

Download the finished video using the available download control.

## Does it use a fixed video template?

The production generator creates a new video from the portraits
and selected directions. It does not simply replace faces in
one of the example videos.

The example videos illustrate possible looks and performances.
They are not promises of an identical result.

## Can I upload a song or write custom lyrics?

This form does not offer custom song uploads or a custom
lyrics field.

It uses preset English rap lines and musical directions.
The generated words, rhythm and timing may vary.

The classic preview uses a fixed original track, but your
generation does not promise that exact track.

## Which tool should I choose?

Choose Hotel Lobby AI for a new musical performance.

Choose [Rumpelstiltskin AI](rumpelstiltskin-ai.md) to personalize
a fixed dance scene.

Choose [photo lip sync](photo-lip-sync.md) when your goal is
to make a portrait speak using your audio or script.
