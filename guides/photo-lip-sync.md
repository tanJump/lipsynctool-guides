# Photo Lip Sync: Make a Portrait Speak

Photo lip sync creates a talking video from a portrait
using an audio or script workflow.

[Explore the photo lip sync guide](https://lipsynctool.com/photo-lip-sync-video-maker)

[Open the LipSyncTool generator](https://lipsynctool.com/#generator)

## Prepare your portrait

Choose a photo with:

- One clearly visible person.
- An unobstructed face and mouth.
- Even lighting.
- Enough detail to identify facial features.

Avoid group photos, covered mouths and heavy filters.
Use a portrait you have permission to animate.

## Choose your speech input

Select the workflow offered by the generator.

For an audio workflow, provide a clear speech recording.
Avoid strong background music, noise and overlapping speakers.

For a script workflow, enter the text and select the
available voice settings.

Supported inputs and settings depend on the selected model.

## Generate the video

Upload the portrait and provide the audio or script.

Review the selected settings and displayed credit cost,
then submit the generation.

Wait for the task to finish before submitting another
request for the same result.

## Review the result

Watch the entire video and check:

- Whether the mouth movement follows the speech.
- Whether the portrait remains recognizable.
- Whether the speech is clear.
- Whether there are unexpected facial movements.

Download the completed video using the available control.

## Improve your next attempt

If the result is unclear:

- Try a sharper, more front-facing portrait.
- Use cleaner audio with one speaker.
- Remove long silent sections from your recording.
- Start with a shorter test.
- Review the selected model's supported settings.
