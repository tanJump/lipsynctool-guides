# LipSyncTool Guides

Practical guides for creating AI lip sync videos, musical duets
and personalized dance clips with LipSyncTool.

This repository contains user documentation.
The application source code is private.

[Visit LipSyncTool](https://lipsynctool.com/)

## Choose your workflow

| Your goal | Tool | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Make a portrait speak with audio or a script | LipSyncTool | [Photo lip sync](guides/photo-lip-sync.md) |
| Create a new musical duet from two portraits | Hotel Lobby AI | [Hotel Lobby guide](guides/hotel-lobby-ai.md) |
| Personalize a fixed dance template with two portraits | Rumpelstiltskin AI | [Rumpelstiltskin guide](guides/rumpelstiltskin-ai.md) |

## Hotel Lobby AI

Upload two portraits, choose a musical style and voice direction,
and generate a new rap duet video.

The tool offers 5-, 10- and 15-second videos, with landscape
and portrait formats.

[Try Hotel Lobby AI](https://lipsynctool.com/hotel-lobby-ai)

## Rumpelstiltskin AI

Assign two portraits to a dancer and a spectator in a fixed
6-second fairy-tale dance template.

Preview the template, choose the roles and generate a video
with the matching template soundtrack.

[Try Rumpelstiltskin AI](https://lipsynctool.com/rumpelstiltskin-ai)

## Photo lip sync

Create a talking video from a portrait using the audio or
script workflow available in the generator.

[Explore photo lip sync](https://lipsynctool.com/photo-lip-sync-video-maker)

## Before generating

- Use photos and audio you have permission to use.
- Choose clear portraits with unobstructed faces.
- Review the displayed credit cost before submitting.
- Check the generated video and audio before sharing.
- AI results may vary in likeness, motion and sound.
